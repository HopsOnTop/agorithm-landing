Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 August 2025 · Last updated: 21 August 2025

Agorithm is a precision agriculture guidance application published by MRT Co, a New Zealand-based company. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Data we collect

Category Examples Purpose
Location data GPS coordinates, heading, speed, altitude from device or external GNSS receiver Live guidance, coverage mapping, and work-log recording
Device information OS version, device model, screen size Compatibility, rendering, and crash diagnostics
User-created content Field boundaries, guidance patterns, machine profiles, task logs Core app functionality and record keeping
Usage analytics Feature usage counts, session duration Product improvement (aggregated, non-identifying)

2. How data is stored

Agorithm is designed offline-first. All user-created content, including field data, guidance patterns, machine profiles, and work logs, is stored locally on your device by default.

If you subscribe to the Pro plan and enable Cloud Sync, a copy of your data is transmitted to and stored on our cloud infrastructure. Cloud-synced data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. You can disable Cloud Sync at any time, and your local data remains unaffected.

3. No account required

Agorithm's Core tier does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or sign in. You can use the app's core features, including GPS guidance, coverage mapping, and calculators, without sharing any personal information.

4. Location data

Agorithm requests location permission solely to provide GPS guidance and coverage mapping during active field work. Location data is:

  • Processed on-device in real time for guidance rendering
  • Stored locally within work logs you create
  • Never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for profiling
  • Never collected in the background when the app is not in active use

5. Third-party services

Agorithm may use the following third-party services:

  • Map tile providers — satellite and terrain imagery is loaded from third-party map services when an internet connection is available. These providers may receive your approximate location to serve the correct tiles.
  • NTRIP correction services (Pro plan) — if you configure an NTRIP connection, your device's approximate position is sent to the correction service you choose. MRT Co does not operate these services.
  • Crash reporting — anonymous crash reports may be sent to help diagnose and fix application errors.

We do not integrate advertising SDKs, social media trackers, or cross-app analytics platforms.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be disclosed only in these circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent (e.g., exporting a work log to share with a client)
  • When required by New Zealand law or a valid legal process
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of MRT Co or others

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. If you use Cloud Sync, you can request deletion of your cloud-stored data by contacting us. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

Agorithm is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and applicable international privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice or on our website. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
