Capabilities

Everything you need in the cab.

Built for real fieldwork on real devices. Works offline, handles landscape and portrait, and runs on the tablet you already have.

GPS Guidance

AB lines, A+ heading, curved patterns, and headland turns. Device GPS, external GNSS, or NTRIP correction services.

Live Coverage

Real-time coverage mapping on satellite imagery. See where you've been, what's left, and export logs when you're done.

Crop Zones

Draw field boundaries and crop zones directly on the map. Export zone data with area calculations and metadata.

Rate Calculators

Spray, seed, and spread rate calculators. Calibration tools, nozzle charts, jug tests, tank mix, and pressure reference.

3D Machine Profiles

True 3D vehicle and implement renders on the work screen. 12 machine types, 6 hitch types, and over 20 implement configurations with geometry-accurate anchoring.

Offline & Zero-Data

Runs entirely without cellular or Wi-Fi. Scout paddocks, record boundaries, and complete full tasks with no connection at all. Cloud sync when you're back in range.

Farm Management

Organise work under a client, farm, and field hierarchy. Group paddocks by property, track jobs per client, and keep records structured from day one.

Pattern Import & QR Sharing

Import guidance lines and field boundaries from other devices. Share patterns between operators instantly with a scannable QR code — no account needed.

Lightweight Mode

Strip back to a minimal interface when all you need is a guidance line and a lightbar. Less on screen, more focus on the pass ahead.

Get started

Field-ready in three steps.

No account required for Core. Set up a vehicle profile and you're in the paddock.

Set up your machine

Pick your vehicle type and implement. The first-run wizard walks you through dimensions, and the geometry renders confirm everything looks right.

Open a task

Tap GO for a quick start, or configure guidance lines, products, and target rates. Load a saved field or start fresh.

Drive

Live GPS guidance with lightbar, real-time coverage, and section control. Finish the task, review the log, and export if you need a record.
